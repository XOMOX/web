1. General information and legal basis

1.1. This privacy policy explains the type, scope and purpose of the processing of personal data on this website (xomox.swiss, the "website"). Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.

1.2. We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Legal bases under the GDPR are in particular Art. 6(1)(b) (contract and pre-contractual measures), (c) (legal obligations) and (f) (legitimate interests, e.g. the secure and stable operation of the website).

1.3. This website is deliberately lean: it sets no cookies, uses no tracking or analytics and embeds no third-party content (no external fonts, scripts, maps or videos). All files (texts, stylesheets, images, scripts) are delivered directly from xomox.swiss. The only exception is the "Book a meeting" page: it asks our booking service for free times and contains a form (see section 4).

2. Hosting and server log files

2.1. The website is hosted by Kreativ Media GmbH, Höschgasse 45, 8008 Zurich, Switzerland. The data is processed in Switzerland.

2.2. Each time the website is accessed, the server automatically stores access data (server log files): name of the page or file requested, date and time of access, amount of data transferred, notification of successful retrieval, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL (the previously visited page), IP address and requesting provider.

2.3. This data is used solely for the security and stable operation of the website (e.g. to detect attacks and broken links). We do not combine it with other data sources or attribute it to specific persons. Log files are kept in line with the host's rules only as long as necessary for these purposes and then deleted; data needed to investigate a specific incident is kept until it has been resolved.

3. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against manipulation, loss and unauthorised access. This includes encrypted transmission between your browser and the server (https).

4. Contacting us and booking meetings

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, to handle an offer or contract. We keep this data for as long as necessary for that purpose or as required by statutory retention obligations.

We use Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, Dublin, Ireland) for email. Microsoft may also process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the EU and the USA. Such transfers are based on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

On the "Book a meeting" page you can book a free time or request a meeting. To do so, the page asks our booking service at xomox.consulting which times are free; for technical reasons your browser transmits your IP address. The booking service runs on a server of IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany, which processes data on our behalf. It sets no cookies. Requests (IP address, time, requested address, browser) are logged in server log files for security and troubleshooting; these are rotated and deleted automatically. To limit misuse, the service also keeps the times of the latest requests per IP address in memory, at most until the service is next restarted.

When you book or request a meeting, we process the details from the form: name, email address, type, length and time of the meeting, your message, the address for on-site meetings and, for requests, the times you suggest. The meeting is entered in our calendar at Microsoft 365, and you receive a calendar invitation from there (for online meetings with a Microsoft Teams link). For this purpose, the details are sent to our booking service and stored there until the meeting has taken place or the request has been dealt with. For the calendar and Teams, the information on Microsoft 365 above applies (processing also in the EU and the USA); Microsoft's privacy policy also applies when you join a Teams meeting. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) and, for the log files, Art. 6(1)(f) (secure and stable operation). We delete the details once they are no longer needed for the meeting and any resulting collaboration, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We only disclose personal data where this is necessary to perform a contract, required by law or you have given your consent. Service providers processing data on our behalf (e.g. the host and the email provider) are contractually bound to data protection.

6. Links to other websites

The website contains links, e.g. to LinkedIn and YouTube. Only when you follow such a link are you taken to that provider's site, where its privacy policy applies. Merely visiting xomox.swiss does not transfer any data to these providers.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request information about the personal data we process about you, as well as its correction, deletion or restriction of processing and the release of your data. You may object to processing at any time. Please contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

8. Changes

We adapt this privacy policy when the website or the legal situation changes. The version published on this page applies.
