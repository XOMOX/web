The promise of transformation – finally delivered

For years we have been told that digitalisation will change companies. Too often it ended with new tools and old ways of working. AI agents change that: they read requirements, plan, implement, test and document – and they work with the systems you already have.

You don't have to throw anything out. Agentic engineering builds on your current environment: your repositories, tickets, pipelines, business applications and file stores.

What agentic engineering means for you

  • Get more done with the team you have. Agents take over recurring work – from drafts to tests to documentation. Your people decide and review.
  • Traceable, not a black box. Every change follows a fixed process of planning, testing, review and approval. Anything risky needs your explicit OK.
  • Tailored to your environment. Not an off-the-shelf product, but a way of working that is built for your company and embedded there.
  • No lock-in. Everything we build lives in your environment and keeps running without XOMOX – with another provider or entirely on your own.

For teams with and without in-house development

Do you have a development team? Then we set up agents to work within your usual process: stories, branches, pull requests, reviews and pipelines.

No in-house development team? Then agents automate processes in your business units – reports, documents, data maintenance, recurring tasks – with clear rules on who approves what.

The package: 3 months of agentic engineering

  1. Environment setup – agents, workspace and rules, set up in your infrastructure
  2. Workflow & governance – from requirement to approval, with risk levels
  3. Team coaching – so your people can lead the agents with confidence
  4. First use case live – a real project, delivered with agents and in operation

CHF 6,000 plus VAT for three months, payable in three monthly instalments of CHF 2,000. Afterwards, ongoing support for CHF 1,000 per month plus VAT if you wish – or you continue on your own.

In practice: how I work myself

In my own work, several agents work in parallel on websites, internal software and billing processes. Every task starts as a story, is planned, implemented, automatically tested and reviewed, and reaches the main branch through a pull request. Changes with financial or external consequences stay switched off until a person approves them. The result: more work in progress in parallel – with every step traceable.
