Agentic engineering

Agentic engineering means AI agents working as part of your team – with clear tasks, fixed processes and human approval. In three months, XOMOX builds this way of working into your company: tailored to your environment, resilient, and able to run without us afterwards.

Your existing systems stay in place

We don't replace anything that works. The agents work with your existing tools – repositories, ticket system, pipelines, business applications, reporting and low-code tools. This turns the long promise of transformation into a concrete next step instead of yet another large-scale project.

The four building blocks

1. Environment setup

We set up the agents' workspace in your infrastructure: access, a secure, isolated execution environment, the structure of your file stores and repositories, and the rules the agents follow – written down and version-controlled. The rules are plain text files and are not tied to a particular AI model or tool.

2. Workflow & governance

Every task follows a fixed process: requirement → plan → implementation → test → review → approval. Specialised agents take on individual steps, people make the decisions. Changes with external or financial consequences are marked as “high risk” and need explicit approval. Connecting your ticket system and your pipelines is part of it.

3. Team coaching

Your people learn to lead agents: describing tasks well, reviewing results, developing the rules further. Regular joint sessions, work on real tasks, no slide-deck training.

4. First use case live

Together we deliver a real project with agents and put it into operation – as proof of the benefit in your environment and as a template for everything that follows.

The three months

Month Focus
1 Assessment, environment setup, first rules and agents, choice of the use case
2 Workflow & governance in everyday work, coaching on real tasks, delivery of the use case
3 Use case live, fine-tuning the rules, handover and documentation – your team can work independently

Who it is for

  • Companies with in-house development: agents work within your usual process with stories, branches, pull requests, reviews and pipelines.
  • Companies without a development team: agents automate processes in business units – reports, documents, data maintenance, recurring tasks – with clear approvals.

Frequently asked questions

What do we need as a prerequisite?

A contact person with time for the joint sessions, access to the systems in question, and the willingness to provide a real process as the first use case. No special AI knowledge is required.

Do we have to replace our existing systems?

No. The agents work with what is there. Where it helps in a particular case, we also include existing reporting or low-code tools.

We don't have our own development team. Is it still worth it?

Yes. The focus is then on processes in your business units rather than on software development. The framework stays the same: clear tasks, fixed processes, human approval.

What happens after the three months?

The package ends. You are free to continue on your own – everything keeps running without XOMOX. Or you choose ongoing support for CHF 1,000 per month plus VAT.

How does the ongoing support work?

A fixed monthly fee with no time tracking. You get in touch when you need something; we develop the way of working further with you. The details are set out in the contract.

Can we keep using what was built without XOMOX?

Yes. Rules, configuration, processes and documentation live in your environment; you can use and develop everything further without restriction. You license AI services directly from the provider. The details are set out in the contract.
