History

Before XOMOX concentrated fully on agentic engineering, three topics were at the centre of its work. The experience gained there goes into every project today: agents work with the systems that are already in place – including reporting and low-code tools.

Digitalisation

Digitalisation can start with paper forms being sent by email. But that is far from using all the advantages of the electronic medium. We helped companies move their processes into the digital world comprehensively – looking first at the process and the people affected by it.

  • transparency and sub-processes running in parallel
  • no duplicate data entry across process boundaries
  • finding all the information you need quickly

Depending on the existing system landscape, we recommended the most suitable platform: often no-code and low-code platforms such as Microsoft Power Platform or Mendix, and BPMN tools for highly structured processes.

Automation

Manual data processing has many weaknesses: procedures are not documented, are hard to standardise and to hand over to other people or systems, errors are handled inconsistently, and redundant steps waste time.

Automation removes these drawbacks. Our approach was to define processes according to standards and to run them event-driven – triggered, for example, by a developer's commit, a user logging on, an entry in a log file or a service starting or stopping.

Reporting

Preparing data sets by different keys supports digital processing in many ways. Reporting turns existing information into formats that can themselves be the source of further analysis – such as XML transformed with XSL or XSL-FO – or presents it as charts and tables.

XOMOX no longer offers these topics as separate services. Where they help in a particular case, we use them as part of agentic engineering.
