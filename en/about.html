About me

I am Peter Wiens, managing director of XOMOX GmbH in Zurich.

For many years I was responsible for IT and drove transformation in organisations – as head of an IT department and as the person responsible for digital transformation. Along the way I saw how big the promise of digitalisation is, and how often it fails because of capacity, complexity and dependence on individual vendors.

With agentic engineering, much of this promise can be kept today. I work this way myself every day: several agents deliver projects in parallel, and every step is planned, tested, reviewed and approved. This is exactly the way of working I build into companies.

What I bring

  • Experience from IT leadership and transformation projects – I know the perspective of management as well as that of IT and business units.
  • Broad knowledge of tools: cloud platforms, DevOps and pipelines, reporting and low-code tools. So nothing in your company needs to be replaced just because it isn't new.
  • A clear stance: what we build lives in your environment and runs without me.
